Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create my first invoice?

Open the app, tap the "+" button, select "New Invoice", fill in your client details and items, then tap "Save".

Can I customize invoice templates?

Yes! Go to Settings > Templates to choose from different designs and customize colors, fonts, and your business logo.

How do I send invoices to clients?

From any invoice, tap the "Share" button and choose "Email". The app will generate a PDF and attach it to your email.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all data is stored securely on your device and in iCloud (if enabled). We don't have access to your invoice data.

Can I backup my invoices?

Yes, enable iCloud sync in Settings to automatically backup your invoices across all your iOS devices.
