Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

🔒 Complete Privacy Guarantee

Your data never leaves your device. We don't collect, store, or have access to any of your invoice data, client information, or personal details.

1. Information We Collect

WE COLLECT NO PERSONAL DATA. Invoice & Estimate iOS App is designed with complete privacy in mind:

Data Stored Locally on Your Device Only:

  • Business information (company name, address, contact details)
  • Client information (names, addresses, contact details)
  • Invoice and estimate data (items, prices, descriptions)
  • App settings and preferences

Data We Do NOT Collect:

  • No personal information
  • No invoice data
  • No client information
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No crash reports sent to us

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Core Functionality: To create, manage, and send invoices and estimates
  • Data Sync: To sync your data across your iOS devices via iCloud
  • App Improvement: To analyze app performance and fix bugs
  • Customer Support: To provide technical support when requested

3. Data Storage and Security

  • 100% Local Storage: ALL invoice data stays on your iOS device only
  • Optional iCloud Sync: If you enable it, data syncs via Apple's secure iCloud (we never see it)
  • Complete Control: Delete the app = delete all your data permanently
  • No Servers: We have no servers storing your data
  • No Cloud Storage: We don't use any third-party cloud services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly share invoices via email or other methods
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights
  • Apple Services: Data shared with Apple for iCloud sync (governed by Apple's Privacy Policy)

5. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with the following services:

  • Apple iCloud: For data synchronization (optional)
  • Apple Analytics: For crash reporting and performance monitoring
  • Email Services: Your device's default email app for sending invoices

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Data Access: All your data is accessible within the app
  • Data Export: Export your invoices as PDF files
  • Data Deletion: Delete invoices individually or reset the app to clear all data
  • iCloud Control: Enable or disable iCloud sync in app settings
  • Analytics Opt-out: Disable analytics in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
